// About Me

Hello and welcome! I’m a passionate software engineer driven by an innate curiosity to uncover unique, innovative solutions to everyday challenges. Currently I am interested and specializing in full-stack engineering along with machine learning workflows. I love working through the development process step by step, from system architecture to scalable backend systems, managing user accounts in a large database, or even trying to get that one UI element to look just right. Even if software can’t always function flawlessly under the hood, I aim to use my knowledge to improve the daily lives of users by bridging complex logic with accessible design.


This website serves as a showcase of my professional journey and the skills I have honed along the way. As you explore, you will find a curated collection of both technical projects and non-technical experiences that highlight my ability to write clean code, collaborate effectively, and adapt to new technologies. Whether building an app from scratch, engaging with the community, or working with a team, I approach every challenge with dedication and willingness to learn. My portfolio displays how I translate my passion for software engineering to tangible real-world impacts, along with how I stay engaged with my local community.
